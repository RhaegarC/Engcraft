### Usage sentences (multiple choice)

| Sentence (blank = answer) | Answer | Why (shown on wrong tap) |
|---|---|---|
| ___ am a student. | **I** | Use I with am — I is the person speaking. |
| Please give the book to ___. | **me** | Use me after a verb or to — it receives the action. |
| Are ___ ready? | **you** | You is the person you are talking to. |
| ___ is my brother. | **he** | He is a boy or man who does the action. |
| I can see ___ over there. | **him** | Use him after a verb or to — a boy who receives the action. |
| ___ is my sister. | **she** | She is a girl or woman who does the action. |
| I like ___ very much. | **her** | Use her after a verb or to — a girl who receives the action. |
| ___ is a small cat. | **it** | It is for animals and things. |
| ___ are good friends. | **we** | We means me and other people together. |
| The teacher teaches ___ English. | **us** | Use us after a verb or to — our group receives the action. |
| ___ are my classmates. | **they** | They is for two or more people. |
| I will help ___ tomorrow. | **them** | Use them after a verb or to — other people receive the action. |

## Possessive Pronouns（物主代词）

### Spelling prompts (Chinese + spoken English)

| English | Chinese prompt |
|---|---|
| my | 我的 |
| mine | 我的 |
| your | 你的 |
| yours | 你的 |
| his | 他的 |
| her | 她的 |
| hers | 她的 |
| its | 它的 |
| our | 我们的 |
| ours | 我们的 |
| their | 他们的 |
| theirs | 他们的 |

### Usage sentences (multiple choice)

| Sentence (blank = answer) | Answer | Why (shown on wrong tap) |
|---|---|---|
| This is ___ pencil. | **my** | Use my + a thing: my pencil. |
| This pencil is ___. | **mine** | Use mine alone, without the thing: this is mine. |
| Is this ___ bag? | **your** | Use your + a thing: your bag. |
| This bag is ___. | **yours** | Use yours alone, without the thing: this is yours. |
| This is ___ bike. | **his** | Use his for a boy or a man: his bike. |
| This is ___ hat. | **her** | Use her + a thing for a girl: her hat. |
| This hat is ___. | **hers** | Use hers alone for a girl: this is hers. |
| The bird is in ___ nest. | **its** | Use its for an animal or a thing: its nest. |
| ___ school is very big. | **our** | Use our + a thing: our school. |
| This classroom is ___. | **ours** | Use ours alone, without the thing: this is ours. |
| ___ house is near the park. | **their** | Use their + a thing: their house. |
| That house is ___. | **theirs** | Use theirs alone, without the thing: that is theirs. |
